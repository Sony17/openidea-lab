# Code of Conduct

We are building a tool for people who want to learn and make things. The community should
feel the same way.

**We expect** contributors to be respectful, assume good intent, give concrete and kind
feedback, and welcome newcomers.

**We do not accept** harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing
others' private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

**Reporting.** Email conduct@openidea.world. Reports go to the maintainers only. We will
acknowledge within three days and act within two weeks. Consequences range from a private
warning to a permanent ban, at the maintainers' judgement.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and is MIT licensed with the
rest of the project.
